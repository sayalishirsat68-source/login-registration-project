Welcome to Udaan Foundation

Our Mission and Vision

Our mission is to empower communities, promote sustainable development, and uplift underrepresented populations. We envision a world where every individual has access to equal opportunities, basic healthcare, quality education, and a dignified life.

Our Story

Founded in 2015, our journey started with a small group of passionate volunteers addressing local community issues. Over the years, we have grown into a fully dedicated NGO, reaching thousands of individuals across multiple regions and driving lasting social change.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity: Operating transparently and responsibly in all actions.
  • Respect: Valuing the diversity and dignity of every individual.
  • Empathy: Understanding and addressing the challenges faced by our communities.

Our Programs

  • Education & Child Support: Providing school supplies, scholarships, and free tutoring.
  • Health & Nutrition: Organizing regular free health checkup camps and clean water distribution.
  • Welfare & Development: Offering skill development and vocational training for women.
